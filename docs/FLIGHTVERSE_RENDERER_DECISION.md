## Result of the spike (the evidence for the favorite)
The spike used a UNIFIED scene (one WebGLRenderer, one scene graph). It had a heightfield terrain from
`dsm_lod256.bin` (256×234, 1.49m/cell, generated by `pipeline/dsm_lod.py`,
126MB→234KB) with the ortho texture, and a 1.51M splat through `GS.DropInViewer`.

| Question of the gate | Result |
|---|---|
| Can one scene hold ksplat and DSM terrain? | YES — 3,137,584 tris (3.02M splat + 117k terrain), 3 draw calls |
| Does the position query work? | YES — bilinear on the heightfield: 10.18m AGL in the center |
| Can the test capture a clean frame? | YES — 1.3MB PNG through a synchronous render and toDataURL (no preserveDrawingBuffer) |
| Do enter and exit work without a leak? | YES — cycle 1 → 0 canvases and 0 contexts; cycle 2 rebuilds with the same performance (1096→1065ms) |
| Are there console errors? | 0 (the only error was a favicon 404, now fixed) |
| First frame | About 1.1s, with the load of the local 36MB ksplat |

## Decision
**three.js r160 + GaussianSplats3D 0.4.7 (DropInViewer), WebGL. Status: CONFIRMED.**
The real risk of the project was not the engine. The risk was how the splat and the terrain work together, and the life cycle of the context.
The spike tested both with numbers. A change of engine costs the 25 points of integration for gains that nobody proved.

## Debts that the spike leaves (they go to the phase backlog)
- The nodata edges of the DSM make visible "teeth". Feather or clip the mask (P2).
- The splat↔terrain alignment is not done. The frames are different: the splat uses a normalized frame and the terrain uses local meters. The raw material is `splats/<id>.cameras.json` (SfM poses, NOT used TODAY). The other input is the centroid offset that `make_viewer_mesh` calculates and discards. SceneManifestV2 must save it (P1, part 2).
- The spike used `progressiveLoad:false`. The real world will use progressive load with a budget.
- Audio, MediaRecorder, and WebCodecs are infrastructure to build from zero (P3/P7). They do not block the engine.
